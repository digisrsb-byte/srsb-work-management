# What's New in v1.2.6

- Added partial-payment recording inside **Edit Recruitment Invoice**.
- Shows Invoice Total, Already Paid, Outstanding, and Current Status.
- Enter Amount Received Now, Payment Date, Payment Method, and UTR / Reference Number.
- Paid and Outstanding values are calculated automatically.
- Status automatically becomes Partially Paid or Received.
- Added Payment History for each invoice.
- Prevents recording more than the current outstanding balance.
